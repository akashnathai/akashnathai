## 👋 About Me

I'm an **AI engineer and researcher** from Silchar, Assam, building AI systems that hold up under real-world constraints: noisy clinical data, low-resource languages, and hardware that fits in your palm. I care about models that are not just accurate, but **explainable and honest about their uncertainty**.

- 🔭 **Now:** AI Researcher on the **Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) project at NIT Silchar**, working on AI that classifies and generates Indian music
- 🏆 **Best Paper Award**, AICTA 2026 (NIT Silchar, Springer LNNS) for *BioSignal-LLM*
- 🎤 First-author oral + e-poster at **WSDM 2025**, Dehradun
- 🎓 B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering, Assam University (2021–2025)
- ⭐ CodeChef 4★ competitive programmer
- 📫 Open to research collaborations, reach me at **akashnath.aus@gmail.com**
